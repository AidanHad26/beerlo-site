FAQ

The usual
questions.

Is BeerLo free?
Yes. Free to download, free to log games, free to run tournaments. No ads, no subscription wall on the core experience.
Is it just for beer pong?
Nope. The name is a wink — BeerLo works for any game where you want to track rankings: pong, spike ball, paddr, darts, cornhole, chess, FIFA, whatever. If it has a winner, you can log it.
How do groups work?
Make a group for your house, friends, fraternity, league — anything. Invite people, log games among the group, climb the group's leaderboard. You can be in multiple groups at once.
What's ELO?
The rating system from chess. Everyone starts at 1000. Win and your rating goes up — more if you beat a stronger player, less if you crush a beginner. It self-corrects over time so the leaderboard reflects skill, not just volume.
Do I need an Android version?
BeerLo is iOS-first for now. An Android version is on the roadmap once we've polished the core experience on iPhone.
